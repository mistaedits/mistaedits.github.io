Privacy Policy — Shorts Kesici

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Shorts Kesici ("the App") is a personal desktop tool operated by Mista Edits ("we", "us"). It helps

the operator turn highlights from live-stream recordings into short vertical videos and publish them

to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. The App runs only on the operator's own

computer; it has no public website, no user registration and no servers of its own.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by its operator to manage the operator's own social media accounts. It is not

offered to the public.

2. Information the App accesses

When the operator connects an account, the App receives only what is needed to publish videos:

PlatformData accessedWhy
YouTube (YouTube API Services)Channel ID and channel name; permission to upload videosShow which channel is connected; upload Shorts
TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API)Open ID, display name, avatar; creator posting settings; permission to upload/publish videosShow which account is connected; let the operator choose privacy and interaction settings; publish videos

The App does not access private messages, followers' personal data, contacts, location, or payment

information, and it does not read or collect data about other users.

3. How the information is used

Information is used only to connect the operator's accounts and to publish videos the operator has

reviewed and approved. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local

folder that is not shared. They are never sent to us or to any third party other than the platform

they belong to (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta).

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the

respective platform's official API when the operator publishes a video.

6. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the

YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the

Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the App's access at any time at

Google security settings — Third-party connections.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

  • YouTube: https://myaccount.google.com/connections
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services → remove "Shorts Kesici"
  • Instagram/Facebook: Facebook → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → remove "Shorts Kesici"
  • In the App: each connected account can be disconnected, which deletes its stored token from the computer.

Stored data is deleted immediately when an account is disconnected or the App is uninstalled.

8. Children

The App is not intended for and is not used by children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
